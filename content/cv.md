## Experience

### Postdoctoral Research Associate, Los Alamos National Laboratory
May 2024 – Present · Los Alamos, NM
{.when}

- Developed [LAMeL](/projects/lamel/), an interpretable meta-learning method for molecular property prediction in data-scarce regimes (up to 25x accuracy gain), published in *Digital Discovery*; extended it to PFAS property prediction; adopted across three LANL divisions.
- Developed a graph neural network architecture using substructure relationship graphs for low-data molecular property prediction (first author, manuscript in preparation).
- PI, LANL LDRD ($60K, 2026): [conditional neural processes](/projects/conditional-neural-processes/) for metal–ligand separation-factor prediction (SAFE database) and epidemic forecasting from agent-based simulation.
- Lead developer of [buhito](/projects/buhito/), open-source graphlet enumeration and analysis code; secured funding as Co-I.
- Built core filtering, parsing and substructure-visualization functionality in [minervachem](/projects/minervachem/).
- Co-organized the TSRC Workshop on Machine Learning and Informatics for Chemistry and Materials (2025, 2026; 20–30 participants); designed and funded 3 Applied Machine Learning Summer School projects (100% acceptance) and mentored 5 graduate students (2025–2026).

### Summer Research Intern, Los Alamos National Laboratory
June 2023 – August 2023 · Los Alamos, NM · mentored by Dr. Nicholas Lubbers
{.when}

- Developed the first meta-learning algorithm for linear models in chemistry, based on a common-subspace assumption and validated on experimental and computational datasets from 3K to 134K data points; became the basis for LAMeL.

### Graduate Research Assistant, University of Utah, Department of Chemistry
May 2020 – May 2024 · Salt Lake City, UT · Prof. Michael Gruenwald Group
{.when}

- Showed that thermodynamic stability poorly predicts cocrystal formation, overturning a standard assumption in pharmaceutical cocrystal design (*JACS* 2024).
- Ran large-scale molecular dynamics on GPU-accelerated HPC: 500K+ particle systems in HOOMD-blue across 200+ concurrent jobs on NVIDIA GPU clusters.
- Built automated Python analysis workflows (pandas, numpy, freud) to extract thermodynamic and kinetic parameters from terabytes of simulation data, reducing analysis time by ~70%.
- Developed modular object-oriented Python packages for molecular dynamics scripting, analysis and visualization.
