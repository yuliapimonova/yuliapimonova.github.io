**Problem.** Screening large molecular libraries needs reliable parsing, filtering and substructure-level tooling around the ML models themselves.

**Method.** I am a core contributor to minervachem, where I built the meta-learning capabilities (including [LAMeL](/projects/lamel/)), filtering pipelines, substructure visualization and data parsing.

**Result.** These modules enable high-throughput screening in molecular search and topology analysis.
