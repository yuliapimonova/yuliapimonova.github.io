**Problem.** Scientific datasets such as metal–ligand separation factors, or forecasts from agent-based epidemic simulations, often offer only a small context of observations per task.

**Method.** We apply conditional neural processes, which learn to predict from a sparse context set, to metal–ligand separation-factor prediction (SAFE database) and to epidemic forecasting from agent-based simulation.

**Result.** I won $60K in internal LANL LDRD funding as PI (2026). First results appeared in the *Proceedings of the AAAI Symposium Series* (2026) and were presented as a poster and spotlight talk at the Parsimonious Scientific Machine Learning Symposium (AAAI Fall Symposium Series). Code is not public yet.
