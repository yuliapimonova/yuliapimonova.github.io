I'm a computational chemist and machine learning scientist, currently a Postdoctoral Research Associate at Los Alamos National Laboratory. I build models that predict molecular properties when data are scarce, using graph neural networks, meta-learning, conditional neural processes and uncertainty quantification, written in PyTorch and run on GPU-accelerated HPC.

My meta-learning method [LAMeL](/projects/lamel/) gives up to 25x accuracy gains in data-scarce regimes and has been adopted across three LANL divisions; I also lead development of [buhito](/projects/buhito/), an open-source library for graphlet-based features on any graph data. In my PhD in physical chemistry at the University of Utah, I used large-scale GPU molecular dynamics to show that thermodynamic stability is a poor predictor of cocrystal formation.
